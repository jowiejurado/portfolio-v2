## Scope
The whole Jowie OS shell (src/os/**) plus built-in apps (src/apps/**). Mode: Experience (portfolio) with Operate-grade shell chrome.

## Audience & job
Recruiters, clients and developers arriving from a link; find projects, experience and contact within seconds while the OS itself proves front-end craft. Content is placeholder (src/content/*), clearly labelled.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The portfolio is an Ubuntu 26.04 desktop, copied faithfully in its chrome (GNOME top bar, left Ubuntu Dock, Yaru headerbars, quick settings, date menu, app grid). It refuses the category default of a scrolling one-page portfolio with hero + cards; content lives in windows you open.
OWN-WORLD: Yaru light/dark tokens (window #fafafa/#2c2c2c, headerbar #ebebeb/#303030), near-black translucent top bar, translucent dark dock, Suru-style squircle app tiles with diagonal fold, pill quick toggles, 12px window radius, ten Yaru accent colours driving --brand. Montserrat everywhere, Ubuntu Mono only in the terminal. Original procedural wallpapers (Resolute bands, Aubergine, Contour, Graphite) painted with the live accent.
STORY: Visitor boots, unlocks, meets a Welcome tour that introduces Jowie and lets them restyle the OS live, then explores About / Projects (App Center pattern) / Experience (timeline) / Contact, or the Terminal and Files for depth.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed accent wallpaper; 32px black top bar (activities pill left, date+time centre, network/volume/battery right); 68px dock left with portfolio apps and Show Apps at bottom; desktop shortcut column; the Welcome window centred with Next as the primary action.
FORM: Concept roll waived by the user's pin — "Copying the exact user interface of the Ubuntu. From buttons, window panel, settings, taskbar etc." (Ubuntu 26.04 Yaru); no seed key. Signature interaction: accent change in Welcome/Settings instantly recolours wallpaper, folders, toggles and focus states.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
